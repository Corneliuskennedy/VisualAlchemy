## 🎯 **TARGET AUDIENCE ANALYSIS**

### **Primary Persona:**
- **Finance YouTubers** met 10K-500K subscribers
- Upload frequentie: 1-3x per week
- Content: Educational finance content (investing, trading, personal finance)
- Pain point: **Time** - spending 20+ hours per video on editing
- Budget: €500-2000 per video is acceptable if it saves time

### **Secondary Persona:**
- **Finance Agencies** managing multiple creator channels
- **Finance Course Creators** needing consistent visual assets
- **Finance Podcasters** wanting to repurpose audio to video

## 💡 **PRO TIPS**

1. **Test one variable at a time** - Don't change everything at once
2. **Let winners run** - Don't kill ads too early
3. **Focus on quality leads** - Better to have 10 good leads than 100 bad ones
4. **Track everything** - Use UTM parameters, conversion tracking
5. **Iterate quickly** - Test, learn, optimize, repeat

---

## 🚨 **COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID**

1. **Too broad targeting** - Focus on finance creators specifically
2. **Weak value proposition** - Lead with time savings, not price
3. **No urgency** - Use "3 spots remaining" or similar
4. **Ignoring retargeting** - Most conversions happen on 2nd+ visit
5. **Giving up too early** - Ads need 7-14 days to optimize
